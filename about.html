About Our University

Zoya Software Engineering University (ZSEU) Is Pakistan's Premier Institute Dedicated Solely To Shaping The Next Generation Of Tech Leaders, Software Architects, And Innovators.

The Visionary Behind ZSEU

From Humble Beginnings to a Tech Empire

Zoya Software Engineering University Is Not Just An Educational Institution; It Is The Realization Of A Lifelong Dream Of An Extraordinary Woman. Born Into A Poor Family In Pakistan, She Faced Countless Socio-Economic Hurdles, But Her Relentless Passion For Technology Led Her To The United Kingdom , Where She Completed Her Advanced Degrees In Software Engineering.

While Studying Abroad, Her Dream Was Always Clear: To Return To Her Homeland And Build A World-Class Tech University For Youth Who Lack Resources But Possess Immense Talent. Through Pure Hard Work, Dedication, And Unshakeable Resilience, Her Dream Finally Became A Reality In 2016 When ZSEU Opened Its Doors.

"Your background does not define your future. Coding is the ultimate equalizer; if you can think, you can build an empire." Chancellor & Founder - ZSEU

Today, She Has Built An Educational Empire That Mentors Thousands Of Students Annually, Bridging The Gap Between Pakistan's Local Talent And The Global Software Industry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know About ZSEU Credentials

Is ZSEU recognized By The Higher Education Commission (HEC)?

Yes, Zoya Software Engineering University Is Fully Recognized By HEC Pakistan And All Degree Streams Are Accredited By The National Computing Education Accreditation Council (NCEAC).

Does The University Offer Evening Classes For Working Professionals?

Yes, We Provide Flexible Executive Weekend And Evening Batches Specifically For Software Engineering And Data Science Modules.
